Observation timeline

Daily war-gate statistic threshold changes and counter deltas for rescues and lost-in-space deaths are derived observations. Actual events and rescue prices come from Pilot.ev. Entries appear as playback reaches their day.

The war-gate statistic is over threshold when there are at least two great-house pairs in total and hostile pairs × 2 ≥ all great-house pairs. This is a daily sample, not a war state or declaration.

    Final run checkpoint hash

    Before playback, this page checks the supplied final checkpoint against this hash and checks every displayed daily frame against a separate digest linked to it. Recorded settings, inputs, and the observation timeline are also bound to the final hash by the timeline digest. These are consistency checks on exporter-supplied evidence; anyone who edits the export can also forge that evidence. They do not authenticate the run or replay the simulation. Metadata is outside these checks. The final hash belongs to the completed export.
